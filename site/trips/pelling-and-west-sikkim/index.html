Pelling & West Sikkim
4 days · 3 nights

Pelling sits on a ridge in West Sikkim facing the full sweep of the Kangchenjunga range. This short trip combines its famous sunrise views with the monasteries, ruins and sacred lake that make the region so special.

Why you will love it
Highlights

  • Sunrise over Kangchenjunga from Pelling
  • Pemayangtse Monastery, one of the oldest in Sikkim
  • The ruins of Rabdentse, Sikkim's second royal capital
  • The sacred Khecheopalri Lake in the forest
  • The glass Skywalk and the giant Chenrezig statue

On the way
Places you'll visit

1 / 2

Day 1-4
Pelling

A small hill town at about 2,150 metres with some of the best mountain views in Sikkim.

1 / 2

Day 2
Pemayangtse & Rabdentse

A 17th-century monastery and the forested ruins of the former royal capital, a short walk apart.

1 / 1

Day 3
Khecheopalri Lake

A wish-fulfilling lake sacred to Buddhists and Hindus, hidden in the forest above Pelling.

Itinerary
Day by day

Day1

Arrival - Pelling

Pick-up at Bagdogra or NJP and drive to Pelling through tea gardens and the Teesta valley (about 6 hours).
Day2

Pelling sightseeing

Sunrise over Kangchenjunga, then Pemayangtse Monastery, Rabdentse ruins and the Skywalk with the Chenrezig statue.
Day3

Khecheopalri Lake & waterfalls

Morning at Khecheopalri Lake, then Kanchenjunga Falls and Rimbi waterfall. Evening free.
Day4

Departure

Breakfast and transfer back to Bagdogra or NJP.
